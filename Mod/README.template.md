RimWorld already lets you give an animal a master and tick "follow master while doing field work". The animal then walks along while your colonist mines, harvests, forages or fishes. It follows, it wanders nearby, it defends — and it never once makes the work go better. This mod makes that walk worth something.

# What it does
When an obedient companion is at hand while its master works, there is a chance the work turns up more than it should: a little extra ore, a few more crops, one more fish, a bit more milk or wool. A mark appears over the animal that found it.

- Mining, harvesting and foraging, fishing, milking and shearing. Each can be switched off on its own.
- The chance rises with training. Mining asks for the "dig" training and harvesting for "forage" — both added by Odyssey, three steps each — and every step earned makes the companion better at it. Fishing, milking and shearing have no training of their own, so obedience is enough there.
- A bonded animal is better still, and working side by side slowly draws the two together.
- Obedience is the floor, and that is the game's own rule: without it an animal does not respect a master at all.

Defaults: 15% base, +10% per training step, +15% when bonded — so a fully trained, bonded companion helps 60% of the time, and turns up a quarter of the usual yield when it does. Everything is adjustable in the mod options.

Deep drills are not affected: nobody stands next to them. Cutting a plant down is not harvesting. An animal fishing on its own is never assisted, and the animal being milked never counts as its own helper.

No data is added to the save: the mod can be added to or removed from a game in progress.

Interface in English and French.
